About the initiative

Why a Study Hour Portal

The Study Hour Portal is an initiative of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of MVJ College of Engineering, Bengaluru, as part of the institution's continuous quality improvement in teaching and learning.

Every faculty member keeps a course page for each subject they teach. The portal brings those pages together, so a student can reach the right material in two taps, whether they remember the teacher, the course code, or only their semester and section.

Course pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and kept up to date by the faculty themselves.

Structured materialSyllabus, notes, slides and question banks for each course, in one format.
Any time, any deviceBuilt for phones first, so the study hour can happen anywhere.
Quality outcomesSupports NBA / NAAC criteria on teaching-learning and student support.

Know your outcomes 🎯

COs, POs and PSOs, in plain words

Every subject you study is planned around outcomes: what you should be able to do at the end. Here is what the three terms mean and why they matter to you.

📘

COs

Course Outcomes

What you will be able to do after finishing one subject. Each subject has 4 to 6 COs, written in its syllabus as CO1, CO2 and so on.

🎓

POs

Programme Outcomes

11 abilities every engineering graduate should have by the end of the B.E. They are set by the NBA and are the same for every branch.

🔌

PSOs

Programme Specific Outcomes

2 abilities that are special to ECE graduates, set by our department.

📘 CO1 to CO5 of every subject➜🎓 11 POs + 🔌 2 PSOs➜🏅 A complete ECE engineer

🤔 Why should you know them?

🗺️ A map for every subjectCOs tell you exactly what you are expected to learn, so you know what to focus on.
📝 Smarter exam prepInternal test and SEE questions are tagged with COs. Revise CO by CO and you won't miss a topic.
📈 Your marks matter twiceThe department uses your marks to measure how well each CO was achieved, and plans extra help where a CO is weak.
💼 Ready for jobs and higher studiesProblem solving, using tools, teamwork and communication are exactly what recruiters and interview panels look for.
